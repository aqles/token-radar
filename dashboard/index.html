How the radar prices a launch

Implied value = TVL × peer multiple × momentum × maturity × chain breadth. The peer multiple is the median market-cap-to-TVL of the token-bearing protocols in the same category, computed from this snapshot — never typed in. Momentum is the 7-day TVL change, clamped and log-scaled so one wild week cannot explode a row. Maturity rewards time spent running with no token, saturating at three years. Chain breadth adds up to 10% for multi-chain protocols.

Implied value is this entry's own claim from the snapshot's figures. It is not a prediction that a token will be issued, or when, or at what price. A protocol is called pre-token only because its record carries no token — its record may still miss one.
