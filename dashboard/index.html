Expected value is the number a farmer decides on — a huge launch that will probably never happen is worth less than a smaller one that likely will. Stablecoins are left out here — their token already trades, so there is no launch to rank — but they stay on the board below, flagged, because the watchlist floor requires them.

How the radar prices a launch

Implied value = TVL × peer multiple × momentum × maturity × chain breadth. The peer multiple is the median market-cap-to-TVL of the token-bearing protocols in the same category, computed from this snapshot — never typed in. A category with exactly two trading peers gets a log-space blend of its own median and the global median, capped at twice the global median, so two observations move the estimate but cannot dominate it. A category with fewer than two peers falls back to the global median: one peer is not a category signal, and in this snapshot the lone peer of a thin category is often a miscategorised outlier. Momentum is the 7-day TVL change, clamped and log-scaled so one wild week cannot explode a row. Maturity rewards time spent running with no token, saturating at three years. Chain breadth adds up to 10% for multi-chain protocols.

Token odds are the chance a protocol ever launches a token, read off its category's own record: the share of the category's protocols that already have one, shrunk toward the snapshot-wide share (19%) with a prior of ten protocols, so a thin category cannot claim certainty. Expected value is the implied launch value times the token odds. Every farm plan is costed — capital plus gas — and the detail view nets the gas against the expected value, so the verdict is a number, not a feeling.

Implied value is this entry's own claim from the snapshot's figures. It is not a prediction that a token will be issued, or when, or at what price. A protocol is called pre-token only because its record carries no token — its record may still miss one. Stablecoins are not launch candidates: their token already exists and trades, pegged. They are left out of the early radar; the two on the watchlist stay on the board, flagged, because the watchlist is the floor.
